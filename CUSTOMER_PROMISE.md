# Customer Promise

We promise to show operational truth.

- We will not invent companies, people, contact details, evidence, replies, meetings, customers, or revenue.
- We will label unknown data, inference, tests, and estimates.
- We will not expose another customer’s targeting, messages, engagement, strategy, or identity.
- We will not send a changed campaign under an old approval.
- We will stop recorded follow-ups after reply, opt-out, bounce, complaint, meeting, or human intervention.
- We will keep AI and paid-provider use bounded and observable.
- We will surface provider and job failures rather than masking them.
- We will provide pause and emergency-stop controls for bounded automation.
- We will not claim universal legal compliance or guarantee sales outcomes.

Customers remain responsible for lawful data use, approved claims, authorized sender identity, business terms, negotiation, commitments, and closing.
