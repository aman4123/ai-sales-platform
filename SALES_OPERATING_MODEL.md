# Sales Operating Model

## Human accountability

The human sales owner approves company knowledge, strategy, audiences, campaigns, sender identity, sensitive replies, prices, proposals, contracts, discounts, and commitments. A human owns meetings and closes opportunities.

## AI workforce

- Nova coordinates bounded jobs and the daily brief.
- Leo retrieves public evidence and records conflicts and unknowns.
- Ava qualifies prospects and prepares sales decisions.
- Maya drafts grounded outreach and enforces approval and suppression gates.
- Alex runs only approved follow-ups and stops on reply, opt-out, bounce, complaint, meeting, or human intervention.
- Ivy maintains tenant-owned CRM truth and deduplicates records.
- Sage triages signed inbound events and creates human-response work.
- Orion reports stored results without inventing performance.

These names are presentation identities. One centrally authorized orchestration and tenant-permission system controls all data access.

## Working loop

1. Save and approve company knowledge.
2. Confirm the sales goal and bounded department controls.
3. Retrieve companies through the configured search provider.
4. Store source URL, title, retrieval time, exact value, verification status, and confidence.
5. Apply explainable qualification and exclusions.
6. Synchronize qualified records into tenant CRM.
7. Draft outreach from approved company knowledge and stored evidence only.
8. Record an immutable recipient, content, sequence, and limits approval.
9. Queue and send only through test mode or explicitly enabled live delivery.
10. Stop future steps on every recorded stop condition.
11. Create human tasks and opportunities where judgment is required.
12. Generate a daily brief from persisted events.

Manual mode requires human approval for external actions. Assisted mode automates low-risk internal work. Autonomous mode executes only a pre-approved strategy within hard limits and never removes the listed human decisions.
