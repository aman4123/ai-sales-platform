# Company Mission

We are building an Autonomous AI Sales Department that can operate our own sales workflow before it is marketed as a proven solution to others.

The product exists to move repetitive sales operations—public-evidence research, qualification, CRM maintenance, approved outreach, follow-up stop rules, reply triage, tasks, and truthful reporting—into bounded, observable automation. Two or three human supervisors should retain strategy, relationships, negotiation, meetings, commitments, and closing.

Our standard is operational proof, not simulated progress. Unknown facts remain unknown. Zero customers and zero revenue display as zero. Test activity remains isolated and labeled. We will never market a capability beyond what production evidence supports.

Our intended position is: “That’s the company that built the AI Sales Department.”
